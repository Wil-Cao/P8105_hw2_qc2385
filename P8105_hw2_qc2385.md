short paragraph about this dataset:

The cleaned NYC subway dataset contains 1868 observations and 19
variables.

The dataset includes subway line and station name, station latitude and
longitude, routes served, entry status, vending availability, entrance
type, and ADA compliance.

These data are not completely tidy because the subway routes are stored
across multiple columns (`route1` through `route11`) rather than in a
single route variable.
